It has come to the attention of the management team, after a considerable amount of discussion and deliberation across several meetings that were held over the course of the past month, that the office kitchen area has, generally speaking, not been maintained in a state of cleanliness that could reasonably be considered acceptable by the majority of the staff who make use of it on a daily basis. In light of this situation, and taking into account the various opinions that have been expressed by numerous members of the team through a variety of channels both formal and informal, it has been decided that, going forward, each department will be assigned, on a rotating weekly basis, the responsibility for ensuring that the kitchen is tidied at the end of every working day, and it is sincerely hoped that this new arrangement will lead to a noticeable and lasting improvement in the overall condition of this shared space for everyone involved.
