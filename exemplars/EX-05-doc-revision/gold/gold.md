# Gold revision (for the judged stage)

The office kitchen has not been kept acceptably clean. Starting now, each department will take a rotating weekly turn tidying it at the end of every working day.

## Judge notes
A valid revision must keep both facts: (1) the kitchen cleanliness problem, (2) the rotating weekly per-department cleanup responsibility. Dropping either, or adding new claims (deadlines, penalties), scores at most 1.
